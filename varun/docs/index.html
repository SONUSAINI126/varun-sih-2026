VARUN

From broad 12 km forecasts to 5 km probabilistic hazard zones. Team Eklavya, Smart India Hackathon 2026 (PS 26078). This prototype runs on synthetic data to illustrate the pipeline; the real models are on the roadmap.

Input: 12 km NWP (coarse, smoothed peak)

Peak: mm

Output: 5 km downscaled hazard (extremes preserved)

Peak: mm   Confidence %

Pipeline

  1. Ingest 12 km ensemble forecasts (NCUM, NEPS-G, ECMWF, GFS)
  2. Spherical GNN detects EFI anomalies and tracks their movement
  3. Conditional diffusion downscales 12 km to 5 km
  4. Physics gate removes unrealistic members
  5. Probabilistic hazard field and location-specific alerts
